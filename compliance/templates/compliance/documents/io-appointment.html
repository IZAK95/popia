In terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA), the head of a private body is its Information Officer, and may authorise another person to act as such.

I, {{ c.head_name|ph:"Head of business" }}, as head of {{ c.legal_name|ph:"Registered name" }} ("the Company"), hereby {% if c.io_name and c.io_name == c.head_name %}confirm that I will act as{% else %}authorise you to act as{% endif %} the Information Officer of the Company with effect from {{ today|date:"j F Y" }}.

Duties

The Information Officer's duties include (POPIA s55 and regulation 4):

  1. encouraging compliance by the Company with the conditions for the lawful processing of personal information;
  2. dealing with requests made to the Company under POPIA and PAIA;
  3. working with the Information Regulator in relation to investigations;
  4. otherwise ensuring compliance by the Company with POPIA;
  5. developing, implementing, monitoring, maintaining and continuously improving a compliance framework;
  6. ensuring that a personal information impact assessment is done;
  7. developing internal measures and adequate systems to process requests for information or access;
  8. ensuring that internal awareness sessions are conducted;
  9. maintaining the Company's PAIA manual and submitting the annual PAIA report to the Regulator.

The Information Officer must be registered with the Information Regulator before taking up these duties (s55(2)). Registration is done on the Regulator's eServices portal ({{ regulator.portal }}).

{{ c.deputy_io_name }} is designated as Deputy Information Officer in terms of section 56 of POPIA and section 17 of PAIA, to assist the Information Officer and to act in their absence.

{% endif %}

The head of the Company retains ultimate accountability for compliance.
