8.4 Security measures

The Company applies appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures to secure personal information, including access control and multi-factor authentication, device encryption, anti-malware and patching, secure backups, physical security of paper records, confidentiality undertakings, written agreements with operators, staff awareness training and a security compromise response plan.

9. How to request access to a record

  1. Complete the prescribed request form (Form 2 of the PAIA Regulations, available from the Regulator's website or from the Information Officer) and send it to the Information Officer at the contact details above.
  2. Provide proof of identity, and, if requesting on behalf of someone else, proof of your authority.
  3. Indicate the form of access required (copy, inspection, electronic) and how you wish to be informed of the decision.
  4. Identify the right you are seeking to exercise or protect and explain why the record is required for that purpose.
  5. The Company will decide within 30 days, which may be extended once by up to 30 days with written notice and reasons.

10. Fees

A requester seeking their own personal information does not pay a request fee. Other requesters pay the request fee prescribed in the PAIA Regulations and, if access is granted, access fees for reproduction, search and preparation as prescribed. The Information Officer will notify the requester of any fee payable and of any deposit required before processing the request.

11. Grounds for refusal and remedies

The Company may refuse access only on the grounds in Chapter 4 of Part 3 of PAIA – for example to protect the privacy of third parties, commercial information of third parties, confidential information, the safety of individuals and property, legally privileged records, or the Company's own commercial information. A requester who is dissatisfied with a decision may lodge a complaint with the Regulator ({{ regulator.paia_complaints }}) or apply to court within 180 days of the decision.
