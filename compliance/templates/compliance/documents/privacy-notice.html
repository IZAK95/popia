5. How we protect it

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including access controls, multi-factor authentication, encryption of devices, secure backups, confidentiality obligations on staff and service providers, and secure destruction of records.

6. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law – for example, tax and accounting records for 5 to 7 years (Tax Administration Act, Companies Act). Afterwards it is securely destroyed or de-identified.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold information about you and request a copy (access);
  • ask us to correct, update or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Contact our Information Officer at {{ c.io_email|ph:"IO email" }}. You may use the prescribed forms (Form 1 for objections, Form 2 for correction or deletion) or simply email, phone or write to us – there is no charge. We will verify your identity before acting.
