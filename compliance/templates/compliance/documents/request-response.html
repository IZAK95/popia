We will respond by no later than [date – 30 days].

B. Extension of the period (access requests)

Dear [Name]

In terms of section 57 of PAIA, we are extending the period to respond to your request [reference] by [up to 30] days, to [new date], because [the request involves a large number of records / a search through records held elsewhere / consultation with third parties]. If you disagree with this extension you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator ({{ regulator.paia_complaints }}) or apply to court.

C. Access granted

Dear [Name]

Following your request [reference], we confirm that we hold the following personal information about you: [describe]. It is used for [purposes] and has been shared with [categories of third parties]. A copy is attached. [Information about other people has been removed to protect their privacy.]

If any of this information is inaccurate or out of date, you may ask us to correct it.

D. Correction or deletion completed

Dear [Name]

As requested ([reference]), we have [corrected / deleted] the following information: [details]. [Where applicable: We have also informed the following parties to whom the information had been disclosed: ...]

E. Request refused (in whole or in part)

Dear [Name]

We have considered your request [reference]. Unfortunately we are unable to [grant access to / correct / delete] [the information / part of the information] because [reason, with the section of PAIA or POPIA relied on, e.g. PAIA s63 – it would disclose personal information of another person; or: the law requires us to keep this record until (date)].

[For a correction request we don't agree with: The information is correct because (evidence). At your request, we have attached a note to the record that you asked for this correction and it was not made. (POPIA s24(2)(c)–(d))]

If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator ({{ regulator.popia_complaints }} / {{ regulator.paia_complaints }}, {{ regulator.portal }}) or apply to court within 180 days.

F. Objection or marketing opt-out confirmed

Dear [Name]

We confirm that we have received your objection ([reference]) and have stopped processing your personal information for [purpose, e.g. direct marketing] with effect from [date]. You will not receive further [marketing communications] from us.

G. Fee estimate (access requests)

Dear [Name]

Thank you for your request [reference]. Confirming whether we hold personal information about you is free. To provide copies of the records, the prescribed access fee is estimated at [R amount], made up of [reproduction / search and preparation time]. [Before we start, please pay a deposit of R amount.] We will provide the records once the fee is paid. (POPIA s23(3))

H. No personal information held

Dear [Name]

Following your request [reference], we have searched our records, including email, electronic files and paper files, and confirm that we do not hold personal information about you. There is no charge for this confirmation. (POPIA s23(1)(a))

I. Processing restricted

Dear [Name]

Following your request [reference], we have restricted the processing of [describe the information] with effect from [date], because [you have contested its accuracy and we are checking it / we only need to keep it as proof / you asked us to restrict it instead of deleting it / you asked us to transfer it to another system]. While it is restricted we will only store it, or use it as proof, with your consent, to protect someone's rights, or in the public interest. We will tell you before we lift the restriction. (POPIA s14(6)–(8))

J. Notice before lifting a restriction

Dear [Name]

We restricted the processing of [describe the information] on [date] ([reference]). [We have finished checking its accuracy and found that (outcome) / The reason for the restriction no longer applies because (reason)]. We will therefore lift the restriction on [date] and resume using the information for [purpose]. If you object, please contact our Information Officer, {{ c.io_name|ph:"Information Officer name" }}, at {{ c.io_email|ph:"IO email" }} before then. (POPIA s14(8))

K. Withdrawal of consent confirmed

Dear [Name]

We confirm that you withdrew your consent to [describe, e.g. processing of your fingerprint for time and attendance] on [date] ([reference]). We have stopped that processing [and deleted the information / and offered you the following alternative: ...]. Processing before your withdrawal remains lawful, and information we need for other lawful reasons, such as your contract or a legal obligation, is not affected. (POPIA s11(2)(b))
