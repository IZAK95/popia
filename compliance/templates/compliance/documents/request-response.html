A. Acknowledgement of receipt

Dear [Name]

We confirm receipt on [date] of your request regarding your personal information (our reference [DSR-reference]). To protect your information, we first need to confirm your identity: [e.g. please reply with a copy of your ID / we will call you on the number we have on record]. We will respond by no later than [date – 30 days].

Kind regards
{{ c.io_name|ph:"Information Officer name" }}, Information Officer, {{ c.legal_name|ph:"Registered name" }}

B. Extension of the period (access requests)

Dear [Name]

In terms of section 57 of PAIA, we are extending the period to respond to your request [reference] by [up to 30] days, to [new date], because [the request involves a large number of records / a search through records held elsewhere / consultation with third parties]. If you disagree with this extension you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator ({{ regulator.paia_complaints }}) or apply to court.

C. Access granted

Dear [Name]

Following your request [reference], we confirm that we hold the following personal information about you: [describe]. It is used for [purposes] and has been shared with [categories of third parties]. A copy is attached. [Information about other people has been removed to protect their privacy.]

If any of this information is inaccurate or out of date, you may ask us to correct it.

D. Correction or deletion completed

Dear [Name]

As requested ([reference]), we have [corrected / deleted] the following information: [details]. [Where applicable: We have also informed the following parties to whom the information had been disclosed: ...]

E. Request refused (in whole or in part)

Dear [Name]

We have considered your request [reference]. Unfortunately we are unable to [grant access to / correct / delete] [the information / part of the information] because [reason, with the section of PAIA or POPIA relied on, e.g. PAIA s63 – it would disclose personal information of another person; or: the law requires us to keep this record until (date)].

If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator ({{ regulator.popia_complaints }} / {{ regulator.paia_complaints }}, {{ regulator.portal }}) or apply to court within 180 days.

F. Objection or marketing opt-out confirmed

Dear [Name]

We confirm that we have received your objection ([reference]) and have stopped processing your personal information for [purpose, e.g. direct marketing] with effect from [date]. You will not receive further [marketing communications] from us.
