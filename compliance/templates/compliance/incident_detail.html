Breach response steps (POPIA s22)

  1. Contain it – immediatelyChange passwords and enable MFA, revoke sessions, remote-wipe lost devices, ask misdirected recipients to delete, isolate infected computers, contact your IT support and the affected service provider.
  2. AssessWhat information, whose, how many people, was it encrypted, what harm could follow (identity theft, fraud, embarrassment)? Record it above.
  3. Notify the Information RegulatorSubmit form SCN1 on the eServices portal (email is no longer accepted since April 2025). Save the reference number here. Notification may only be delayed if a public body or the Regulator determines it would impede a criminal investigation.
  4. Notify the affected people in writingBy email, letter to their last known address, a prominent notice on your website, or news media. It must give enough information to let them protect themselves: possible consequences, what you've done, what they should do, and the identity of the unauthorised person if known (s22(4)–(5)). Use the notification letter template.
  5. Fix the cause and learnRecord the root cause and what you changed (e.g. MFA, encryption, training). Update the risk register.
  6. Close the incidentKeep this record (retention: 5 years) as evidence of your response.
