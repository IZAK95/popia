Who is it?

For a private business, the Information Officer is automatically the head: the sole proprietor, the CEO/managing director of a company, or the partner/person in charge. The head may authorise another employee to be the Information Officer (in writing), but the head stays accountable. In a small business, the owner is usually the IO.

What do they do?

  • Encourage and oversee compliance – this framework.
  • Handle requests from people and PAIA access requests.
  • Work with the Regulator during any investigation.
  • Ensure a personal information impact assessment is done (your risk register).
  • Develop internal measures and run staff awareness sessions.
  • Continuously improve the framework (added by the 2025 amendments).

Registration

The IO may only take up their duties once registered with the Information Regulator (s55(2)). It's free and online:

  1. Go to eservices.inforegulator.org.za and create a profile.
  2. Select Information Officer registration and enter the business details (including CIPC registration number) and IO details.
  3. Keep the confirmation and record the date in your company profile.

Use the same profile later to report breaches and submit the PAIA annual report. Tip: register with a role email such as privacy@yourdomain so it survives staff changes.

Deputy Information Officers

Optional for small businesses. Useful if someone needs to handle requests when the IO is unavailable. Deputies are designated in writing and also registered.
