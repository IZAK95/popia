People whose information you hold have enforceable rights. A request can arrive by email, phone, WhatsApp, letter or in person – since April 2025 you may not insist on a specific form, and you may not charge for objections or correction requests.

The rights

  • Access (s23 + PAIA): to know whether you hold information about them, get a description or copy of it, and know who has had access to it.
  • Correction or deletion (s24): where information is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained – or no longer authorised to be kept.
  • Objection (s11(3)): to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time.
  • Restriction (s14(6)–(8)): in some cases you must keep information but stop using it, for example while you check information they say is wrong, or when they'd rather you restrict unlawful processing than delete the information. You must tell them before you lift a restriction.
  • Withdraw consent (s11(2)(b)) at any time, where you rely on consent. What you did before stays lawful.
  • Not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal effect (s71).
  • Complain to the Information Regulator, and sue for damages (s99).

How to respond

  1. Log it in Requests the same day. The app sets a 30-day deadline.
  2. Verify identity before disclosing anything – sending information to an impostor is a breach.
  3. Acknowledge the request.
  4. Search email, shared drives, HR/payroll, accounting and paper files.
  5. Decide within 30 days. For access requests you may extend once by up to 30 days, in writing with reasons (PAIA s57). If you don't respond in time, the request is deemed refused.
  6. Respond in writing using the letter templates. You may redact other people's information. Refuse only on grounds in PAIA (e.g. another person's privacy, legal privilege, third-party commercial information).

Fees

People requesting their own personal information pay no request fee. Objections and correction/deletion requests are free. For other PAIA requests prescribed fees apply (see your PAIA manual).
