Key changes as at September 2026 – all built into this app.

April 2025 – POPIA Regulations amended

  • Information Officers must not only develop but continuously improve the compliance framework (regulation 4). The duty to maintain the PAIA manual moved out of the POPIA regulations but still applies under PAIA.
  • Objections and correction/deletion requests may be submitted in a form substantially similar to Forms 1 and 2, by many channels (hand, email, SMS, WhatsApp, recorded phone call), and free of charge.
  • Direct marketing consent must be in a form substantially similar to Form 4, free, specific about the goods/services and channel – and an opt-out is not consent.
  • Broader grounds for who may complain to the Regulator and a streamlined complaints process.

1 April 2025 – Breach reporting via eServices only

Security compromise notifications must be submitted on the eServices portal (form SCN1).

December 2024 – Guidance Note on Direct Marketing

Clarifies that email, SMS and phone marketing require opt-in consent (or an existing customer relationship) and that every message must include an opt-out.

March 2026 – Health Information Regulations

Regulations on the processing of health information came into force on publication (6 March 2026) with no transition period. They apply to insurers, medical schemes, pension funds and employers, and emphasise confidentiality, safeguards and transparency when handling sick notes, medical and incapacity information.

Coming

  • A Guidance Note on transborder information flows (cross-border transfers, cloud computing) is being developed by the Regulator. Until then, document a s72 basis for every overseas provider – which the Operators register helps you do.
  • The PAIA annual report window runs every year from 1 April to 30 June.

Researched September 2026 from Information Regulator notices and law-firm summaries (Bowmans, Baker McKenzie, Michalsons, Werksmans and others). Laws change – check the Regulator's website yearly as part of your review.
