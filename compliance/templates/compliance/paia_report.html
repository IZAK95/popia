PAIA annual report

Every private body must report to the Information Regulator each year on requests for access to records (PAIA s83(4)) – even if you received none. Submit on the eServices portal between 1 April and 30 June. The report covers 1 April to 31 March, regardless of your financial year.

How to submit

  1. Sign in to eservices.inforegulator.org.za with the profile you used to register the Information Officer.
  2. Choose the PAIA annual report (section 83(4)) option for private bodies.
  3. Answer the questions using the figures on this page. If you had no requests, report zeros.
  4. Save or screenshot the confirmation and mark the task done in Tasks.
