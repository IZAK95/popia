  1. Verify the person's identityConfirm they are who they say they are (e.g. call back on the number on file, or ask for a copy of ID). Never send information to an unverified requester (s23).
  2. Acknowledge receiptLet them know you received the request and when to expect an answer. Use the response letter templates. Requests may arrive by email, phone, WhatsApp or letter and may not be refused because they're not on the official form.
    3. {% if object.request_type == "access" %}
  3. Find the informationSearch email, files, HR/payroll, accounting and paper files. Tell them what you hold, who it was shared with, and give copies (you may redact other people's information).
  4. Decide within 30 daysGrant in full or in part, or refuse only on a PAIA ground (e.g. s63 another person's privacy, s64 commercial information of a third party, s67 legal privilege). You may extend once by up to 30 days if you tell them in writing why (PAIA s57).
    5. {% elif object.request_type == "correction" or object.request_type == "deletion" %}
  5. Check the requestIs the information inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained? Check the retention schedule – some records must be kept by law.
  6. Correct / delete, or explainDo it, or attach a note that correction was requested but not made and why. Tell anyone you shared the information with, if the change affects decisions about the person (s24).
    7. {% elif object.request_type == "objection" or object.request_type == "marketing" %}
  7. Stop the processingAfter a valid objection you may no longer process the information for that purpose (s11(4)). Marketing opt-outs must always be honoured – add them to your do-not-contact list.
    8. {% else %}
  8. InvestigateLook into the concern and involve the Information Officer.
    9. {% endif %}
  9. Respond in writing and closeRecord the outcome and date here. Tell them they may complain to the Information Regulator if unhappy (eServices portal, or POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za / PAIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za).
