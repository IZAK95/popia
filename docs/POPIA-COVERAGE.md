# POPIA coverage map

How the app lets a **private body** (a business, sole proprietor or non-profit) meet each obligation that the
Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 places on it, and keep evidence that it did. Checked against the
Act as published in Government Gazette 37067 (26 November 2013). Items like `GOV-01` are checklist items in the app.

The app gives you the framework, the records and the documents. Compliance still depends on doing the work: signing
the agreements, publishing the notices, putting the security measures in place and registering your Information
Officer. The Regulations (as amended in 2025), the 2026 Health Information Regulations and PAIA add duties on top of
the Act; see [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

## Chapter 4: exemptions (s36–s38)

Only apply if the Regulator grants an exemption (s37) or you perform a listed public-protection function (s38). No
action is needed unless you rely on one; record it on the processing activity if you do.

## Not covered

- **Public bodies.** The app and its documents are written for private bodies. Public bodies have extra duties
  under PAIA and different Information Officer rules.
- **Businesses whose core activity needs prior authorisation** (credit bureaus, or processing criminal records for
  others). SPEC-08 flags this, but the application to the Regulator is outside the app.
